Why this exists

The Indian church deserved better than a mobile website with bad fonts and missing songs.

For years, worship leaders in Hindi-speaking churches projected lyrics from cluttered PDFs and PowerPoint files, or scrolled through Facebook posts mid-service. Apps existed — but they were built for English worship, with English fonts. Hindi was an afterthought. Devanagari rendered like a fax. Hinglish was missing entirely. We had to build it ourselves.

Three steps

Open, install, present.

01

Open in any browser

vandanaapp.vercel.app — works on iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows. No App Store. No Play Store. No login.

02

Add to Home Screen

iOS Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android Chrome → ⋮ menu → Install. Desktop → install icon in the address bar.

03

Build a setlist, hit Present

Pick tonight's songs. Tap each to add to setlist. When the band starts, hit Present — block-by-block lyrics, full-screen, in either script.
