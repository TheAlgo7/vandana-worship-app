## Design Context

### Users
Indian Christian congregation members and worship leaders. Used primarily during church services (dark rooms, projected screens, standing), and personal late-night prayer/devotion on phones. Audience spans Hindi and Hinglish speakers; significant portion reads Devanagari. Worship leaders need the Present mode quickly. Congregants search for songs by title or artist mid-service.

### Brand Personality
Sacred · Intimate · Quiet confidence. Not a music streaming app — a devotional tool. The silence between notes matters as much as the notes.

Three words: **reverent, unhurried, warm**.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Sacred Noir** — deep black backgrounds (`#0A0A0E`), warm gold accent (`#C4AA7E`), Lora serif for display text. Dark-first because the primary use context is dim church halls. The gold is used sparingly — it is sacred light, not decoration. Custom brand font "Cathez" for the app title only.

Anti-references: no streaming-app aesthetics (Spotify green, neon), no glassmorphism-everywhere, no hero metrics, no card grids. This is not a tech product — it is closer to a leather-bound hymnal.

### Design Principles
1. **Silence is design** — empty space, restraint, and muted tones serve the worship context. More whitespace, fewer decorative elements.
2. **Gold is sacred** — the accent (`--accent`) should appear at meaningful moments only: active states, Scripture, section labels. Never as decoration.
3. **Text is primary** — lyrics are the product. Typography and readability trump visual flair on every screen.
4. **Dark by default** — church halls are dim. Brightness should not distract during worship.
5. **One hand, one thumb** — all critical interactions (search, navigate, present) must be reachable thumb-zone in portrait orientation on a 6-inch phone.
